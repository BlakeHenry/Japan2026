**Recommendation: highest priority.** Osaka's Shinsaibashi flagship pairs a large official shop with the permanent Pokémon Cafe on the 9th floor of Daimaru Shinsaibashi. The full-scale Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres display supplies the required ceremonial grandeur. The center currently operates 10:00–20:00 and connects directly to Shinsaibashi Station Exit 4.

The cafe is reservation-only. Monitor the official reservation page when October inventory is released; merchandise browsing itself requires no reservation.
