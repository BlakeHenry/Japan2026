**Recommendation: approve for collectors.** A Pokémon-only card shop seven minutes from Kyoto Station with modern, expanded, old-back, and special singles, plus supplies and 42 play seats. It opens 12:00–21:00 on weekdays and 10:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays.

This is the better venue for deliberate singles procurement; the official Pokémon Center should not be asked to perform the duties of a secondary market. Compare condition and price before authorizing expenditure.
