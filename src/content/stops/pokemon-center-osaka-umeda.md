**Recommendation: conditional approval.** The official Umeda store occupies LUCUA SOUTH's 13th-floor Characters World, five minutes from JR Osaka Station, with a permanent Pokémon Card Station for products, events, free play, and rules advice. It currently operates 10:00–20:00; Nintendo Osaka is on the same floor.

Excellent when the itinerary already passes through Osaka Station. It does not, by itself, justify a dedicated cross-city deployment after Osaka DX and Namba have been completed.
