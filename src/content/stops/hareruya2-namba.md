**Recommendation: approve as the Osaka TCG anchor.** This Pokémon-only specialist is two minutes from Nankai Namba Station and carries modern, expanded, old-back, and special singles, plus supplies. It has 80 free play seats and daily events; hours are 12:00–21:00 on weekdays and 10:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays.

Begin the Nipponbashi card-shop circuit here, then compare nearby Ota Road stores only if morale and budget remain intact. Mystery packs are entertainment, not an investment thesis.
